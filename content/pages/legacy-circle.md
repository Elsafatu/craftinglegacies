The Legacy Circle is the readership of Crafting Legacies. Joining means new essays, commentary and research arrive by email as they are published.

## What arrives

- New writing, in full, as soon as it goes out
- Occasional notes on work in progress and questions being wrestled with
- Nothing else — no forwarded newsletters, no promotions, no filler

The rhythm follows the writing rather than a schedule. Some months bring several pieces; some bring none. Publishing on a timetable tends to produce writing that exists because the timetable said so.

## Who it is for

Anyone thinking seriously about what they are building and what becomes of it — families holding property across generations, business owners approaching succession, professionals working in land, governance or estates, and readers who simply want to understand the systems they are already inside.

## Your details

Your email address is used to send you the writing, and for nothing else. It is never sold, shared or passed on. You can leave at any time using the link at the bottom of any message.
