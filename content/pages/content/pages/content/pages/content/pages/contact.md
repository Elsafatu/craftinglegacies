## What this site is

The writing published here is commentary and general discussion. It is not legal advice, and reading it does not create an advocate and client relationship. Every property, estate and governance question turns on its own facts, and the right answer for one situation is frequently the wrong answer for another.

If something here raises a question about your own circumstances, that is the point — but the next step is a conversation, not an article.
